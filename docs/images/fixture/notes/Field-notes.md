# Field notes — cover art

Ideas for the chapter openers. Each one gets a generated image, saved next to
this note so the recipe stays with the picture.

- A lighthouse on a rocky shore, early morning fog
- An empty reading room, low winter sun through tall windows
- A market stall at dusk, paper lanterns

The seed is the important part: it is what makes a picture repeatable. Once a
result looks right, the seed and the prompt travel together in the note's
frontmatter.
